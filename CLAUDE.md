## How to work with me
- Before making changes, briefly explain your plan and why you chose this approach over alternatives.
- After each change, summarise what changed, why, and what could break (edge cases, security, performance).
- Flag anything touching auth, payments, user data, or database schema so I review it line by line.
- Write or update tests for any logic change, and tell me what each test proves.
- If I ask "explain", teach me the concept, don't just restate the code.
